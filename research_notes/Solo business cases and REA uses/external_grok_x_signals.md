signals, last ~90 days as of 12 Oct 2026) and pasted into this conversation.

## Price complaints / "would pay" signals
- Direct organic complaints from founders/SMBs naming specific tool prices were scarce.
  Most relevant posts came from accounts pitching competing tools (@polsia / @newonpolsia)
  that restate the pain.
- @polsia (26 Aug 2026): "A 15-person startup can't afford a $150K compliance officer. But
  without SOC 2, it can't close enterprise deals." Pitched a cheaper readiness tool.
  https://x.com/polsia/status/2092463280568832228
- @polsia (3 Aug 2026): "Drata. Vanta. $5K-$15K a year for what should be a continuous,
  automated job." Pitched an indie-priced alternative.
  https://x.com/polsia/status/2084426759030948156
- @polsia / @newonpolsia (Aug–Oct 2026): repeated posts on small businesses unable to afford
  accessibility specialists or $20k audits; pitched done-for-you ADA/SOC tools at
  $1k–1.5k/mo or self-check kits.
  https://x.com/polsia/status/2090553359677993140
  https://x.com/newonpolsia/status/2107468114560716881
  https://x.com/newonpolsia/status/2109277718580023415

  fees, surfacing mid-negotiation. https://x.com/Startup_Fortune/status/2106433605249708324

## Grok's inference
The loudest X signals are builders restating the cost problem for SOC 2 and ADA rather
than buyers complaining: consistent with a real gap, but weak evidence of ready-to-pay
demand at scale, and a sign these niches already attract many indie competitors.
