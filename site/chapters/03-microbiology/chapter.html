Cleanrooms do not forgive a sloppy record.

Moved into cGMP manufacturing to learn quality at its strictest. Sterile isn't the same as safe. I tested purified water, WFI and pure steam for endotoxin, each sample beside a spiked twin to prove nothing in the water was hiding it. I sampled points of use inside cleanrooms, identified isolates by VITEK 2, and carried a facility-wide disinfectant efficacy study from execution through the co-authored report. The UF master's, finished while working full time, made my microbiology match my chemistry.

Methods · endotoxin, IDs, disinfectants

Endotoxin, kinetic chromogenic (USP <85>), on purified water, WFI and pure steam. Point-of-use water sampling, including cleanrooms (USP <1231>). Identification by VITEK 2 (USP <1113>). Disinfectant efficacy (USP <1072>). {{fig:disinfectant}}: dried S. aureus ATCC 6538 on two 2 × 2 in 316L coupons (the alloy in {{figref:field}}). One is treated for the protocol's predetermined contact time (10 min), then both are neutralized in {{fact:micro.key.recoveryMl}} mL and {{fact:micro.key.platedMl}} mL is pour-plated on TSA with lecithin and polysorbate 80, 30–35 °C, 72 h. Neutralizer effectiveness verified (USP <1227>). Control read at the dilution with the highest count under 250 (USP <61> plate rule). CFU per coupon = CFU/mL plated × dilution factor × {{fact:micro.key.recoveryMl}} mL. Control {{fact:micro.key.control.colonies}} × 103 × {{fact:micro.key.recoveryMl}} = {{sci:micro.key.control.cfuSci}} (log {{fact:micro.key.control.log}}). Disinfected {{fact:micro.key.treated.colonies}} × 1 × {{fact:micro.key.recoveryMl}} = {{fact:micro.key.treated.cfu}} (log {{fact:micro.key.treated.log}}). Log reduction {{fact:micro.key.control.log}} − {{fact:micro.key.treated.log}} = {{fact:micro.key}}; criterion cited by USP <1072>: 3 log for vegetative bacteria, 2 for spores. LOD {{fact:micro.key.lod}} CFU per coupon (1 colony × {{fact:micro.key.recoveryMl}} mL ÷ {{fact:micro.key.platedMl}} mL): an empty plate is reported as less than {{fact:micro.key.lod}}, never zero, and the reduction as more than 5.27.
