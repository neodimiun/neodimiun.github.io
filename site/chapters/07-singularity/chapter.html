When does the wait hit zero?

I track how close we are to recursive self-improvement: AI that builds better AI faster than people can check it. Each dot is a milestone; its height is the wait until the next one. Kurzweil's table runs from life on Earth to the personal computer; my list adds {{fact:sing.data.picks}} AI model releases since {{fact:sing.data.firstYear}}. On my list the waits keep shrinking. Every week I refit where they reach zero.

Methods · data, fit, range

Data. Ray Kurzweil's “Countdown to Singularity” table in The Singularity Is Near (2005): {{fact:sing.data.kurzweil}} milestones, each placed in years before {{fact:sing.asof}} with his time to the next one. My hand-picked list: {{fact:sing.data.picks}} AI model releases, {{fact:sing.data.firstModel}} ({{fact:sing.data.first}}) to {{fact:sing.data.lastModel}} ({{fact:sing.data.last}}). I fixed the list for the fit on {{fact:sing.data.frozenOn}} at {{fact:sing.data.frozen}} releases; the {{fact:sing.data.added}} added later are drawn but not fitted.

Wait. The days from a release to the next release date on the list. The last wait is still open: {{fact:sing.data.open}} days so far, a lower bound, drawn hollow with an arrow up.

Fit. Least squares of ln(wait) on u and u2, with u = ln(days left to the omega point), over Kurzweil's {{fact:sing.data.kurzweil}} rows and the {{fact:sing.data.waits}} closed waits between releases on the fixed list ({{fact:sing.data.n}} points): ln(wait) = a + b·u + c·u2, a = {{fact:sing.fit.a}}, b = {{fact:sing.fit.b}}, c = {{fact:sing.fit.c}}. Quadratic while c is negative, so the curve bends down to zero wait at the omega point; otherwise a straight line. Solid up to the snapshot, dashed after it. On {{fact:sing.heldout.n}} held-out waits, each predicted from the ones before it, a plain exponential scores better than this curve: mean log error {{fact:sing.heldout.exp}} against {{fact:sing.heldout.quad}}.

Omega point. The date the refit of {{fact:sing.omega.refit}} names, {{fact:sing.omega}}, {{fact:sing.omega.days}} days after the snapshot: where the curve fits best when the open wait since the last release on the fixed list ({{fact:sing.omega.open}} days, a lower bound) also counts. The curve drawn here is refit to the {{fact:sing.data.n}} points with that date held. {{fact:sing.refits.kind}} on {{fact:sing.refits.refits}}, each with only the releases before its day, named {{fact:sing.refits}}: it keeps moving {{fact:sing.refits.drift}}. Beside the as-of date: the days from the snapshot to that date, and what the same count was at the {{fact:sing.asof.prevRefit}} refit.

50 % range. tl = the last release on the fixed list on or before the refit day, {{fact:sing.range.tl}}; R = omega point − tl = {{fact:sing.range.R}} days. The range runs from tl + {{fact:sing.range.lower}} R = {{fact:sing.range.lo}} to tl + {{fact:sing.range.upper}} R = {{fact:sing.range.hi}}; both multipliers are the middle half of {{fact:sing.range.n}} held-out errors in a rolling backtest (provisional, in-sample).
